Charts in the main popover

The popover is fixed at 560pt, so any chart above the list pushes apps down. Each option is shown for three ranges (24h, 1W, 6M) at real size. Chart buckets change with the range: 15 min for 6h, 30 min for 12h, hourly for 24h, daily for 1W and 1M, weekly for 6M. Hover the bars for tooltips. The app detail view keeps its own chart either way.
